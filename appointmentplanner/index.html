        How it works

        • Time runs in 1-hour slots. Appointments start on the hour and last 1 to 4 hours.
        • Default hours are . Change the default days and hours in Settings. Use Adjust hours to change a single day.
        • Moving is allowed up to the day before. An appointment can be moved until the end of the previous day. On the day itself it's locked.
        • Drag an appointment to a red-outlined slot, or open it and choose Move.
        • Link appointments to a job to track work on site. Set On site to Delayed to note it on the job, or to Client signed off to mark the job Completed, ready for the balance invoice.
